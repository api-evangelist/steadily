# Steadily

> Steadily is fast and affordable landlord insurance — built by landlords, for landlords.
> We write policies for rental property owners in all 50 US states, with online quotes
> in a couple of minutes and coverage designed around how rental properties actually work.

Steadily was founded by a landlord who couldn't find decent insurance for his own rental
property, and the product reflects that origin: policies cover the perils landlords
actually face (fire, storm and hail, water, vandalism and burglary, riot and civil
commotion), plus landlord-specific protections like loss of rent and legal liability
that homeowners policies don't address well. Steadily insures long-term and short-term
residential rentals — single-family homes, multifamily up to 4 units, ADUs, condos,
and vacant or under-restoration properties. Steadily does not currently write commercial
property or buildings with 5+ units.

Steadily also partners heavily with independent insurance agents, who can quote and bind
Steadily policies through a dedicated agent portal.

## Coverages

Steadily's seven core coverages for landlord policies:

- [All landlord coverages](https://www.steadily.com/landlord-coverages): Overview of every peril and protection included in a Steadily policy.
- [Fire](https://www.steadily.com/landlord-coverages/fire): Structural fire damage, smoke damage, personal property the landlord owns at the unit, and lost rental income during repairs.
- [Legal liability](https://www.steadily.com/landlord-coverages/liability): Medical bills, legal defense, and settlements when a tenant or visitor is injured at the rental. Included in every Steadily policy in all 50 states, with $300K to $2M per-occurrence limits.
- [Loss of rent](https://www.steadily.com/landlord-coverages/loss-of-rent): Replaces lost rental income when a covered event makes the property temporarily uninhabitable.
- [Riot and civil commotion](https://www.steadily.com/landlord-coverages/riot-civil-commotion): Property damage from organized civil unrest. Documentation connecting damage to the unrest is required.
- [Storm and hail](https://www.steadily.com/landlord-coverages/storm-and-hail): Structural damage from wind, hail, tornadoes, and storms.
- [Vandalism and burglary](https://www.steadily.com/landlord-coverages/vandalism-burglary): Damage and loss from break-ins and isolated vandalism. A police report is typically required to file a claim.
- [Water](https://www.steadily.com/landlord-coverages/water): Sudden water damage from burst pipes, plumbing failures, and appliance overflow. Flood damage from rising water requires a separate flood policy.
